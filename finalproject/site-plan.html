Explore Cagayan Valley

Individual Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Site Name: Explore Cagayan Valley

Reason for Selection: This name was chosen because it provides a clear, inviting, and direct call-to-action for visitors while establishing the regional scope of the project. It explicitly targets tourists and travelers looking to explore all five provinces of Region 2 in the Philippines (Batanes, Cagayan, Isabela, Nueva Vizcaya, and Quirino).

Optional Available Domain: explorecagayanvalley.org

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Explore Cagayan Valley is to provide a comprehensive, centralized digital portal for regional tourism in Region 2. The site offers the following services and information:

  • Destination & Attraction Guides: Filterable profiles of natural wonders, historical landmarks, eco-tourism sites, and beaches across all 5 provinces with commuting guides and entrance fee details.
  • Accommodations & Dining Directory: A searchable directory listing accredited hotels, resorts, homestays, and local restaurants/eateries (highlighting local dishes like Pancit Batil Patung).
  • Local Commodities & Souvenir Market: A dedicated section spotlighting local crafts, specialty food products, and souvenir shops (Pasalubong centers) to support local micro-enterprises and vendors.

3. Scenarios

The target audience includes domestic travelers, foreign tourists, and local commuters. Typical scenario questions driving the website content include:

Scenario 1: "Where can I find accredited homestays in Batanes or affordable hotels near Callao Cave in Cagayan with rates under ₱2,500 per night?"

Scenario 2: "What are the top eco-adventure destinations in Quirino and Nueva Vizcaya, and how do I commute to them from Tuguegarao or Santiago City?"

4. Color Scheme

The selected color palette reflects the coastal waters, agricultural landscapes, and mountain ranges of Cagayan Valley:

Color Application:

  • Ocean Deep Blue (#1A365D): Used for headers, section title backgrounds, main navigation bars, and primary buttons.
  • Emerald Forest Green (#2F855A): Used for call-to-action buttons, active navigation links, subheadings, and link hover states.
  • Off-White Neutral (#F7FAFC): Used for the main body background and card container backgrounds for clean contrast and readability.
  • Charcoal Gray (#2D3748): Used for main paragraph body text, directory details, and body text elements.

5. Typography

The site plan and upcoming website utilize two web fonts to ensure visual hierarchy and mobile readability:

Heading Font: Montserrat

Montserrat Bold (600 / 700)

Usage: Applied to main site titles, page section headings (<h1>, <h2>, <h3>), hero banners, and primary navigation links.

Body Font: Open Sans

Open Sans Regular / Semi-Bold (400 / 600)

Usage: Applied to all body text paragraphs, directory list items, form labels, table text, and footer information.
